# Village inn stalls and vsync-on frame times, 2026-10-27

Commit ea96be8 (main 818dda5 plus the probe's 'agents' fix), `npm run build`, WebGPU, SHOT_GPU=1, 1920x1080.
Machine: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 (monitor at 60 Hz, 4096x2160) and an AMD Radeon iGPU (59 Hz, 2560x1440). Headless Chromium.
Waited 30 s with no other probe/shot/build job before each run.

## Summary

- **Vsync on (like shot.mjs), 10 s of rAF intervals:** both scenes run at a steady ~17.5 ms (about 57 fps) with p95 18.1-18.2 ms, so they **pass the Gate B rule (p95 <= 25 ms)** with a wide margin. There is one hitch per 10 s: 35 ms in the town, 52-54 ms in the inn. Two runs agree.
- With vsync on, the interval is ~17.5 ms, not the 16.7 ms of a 60 Hz display. Headless Chromium drives frames from its own BeginFrame timer, not the monitor.
- **The 45-in-8-s inn stalls from this morning were caused by the probe's own `agents` error.** On ea96be8 the probe reads village inn 8.5 ms p50 (was 11.1) with 3 of 996 frames over 25 ms (was 45 of 564). Town noon reads 10.9 ms p50 (was 11.4) with 0 of 743 over 25 ms.
- **What is left: rare long tasks when a townsperson is dressed.** A 10 s trace (vsync off) has 2 rAF gaps over 26 ms (71 ms and 33 ms, 1.85 s apart, both in the first 4 s, then none for 6 s). A 6 s trace showed the same: 65 ms and 36 ms, 1.9 s apart.
  - Renderer main thread: busy for the whole gap (71.0 of 71.1 ms, 32.6 of 32.8). The work runs in microtasks queued from a worker message (`HandlePostMessage` 32 ms, with `RunMicrotasks` chains of 4-8 ms), outside the rAF callback. That is why the probe's per-frame spans didn't show it.
  - CPU samples: `FolkView.prepareSlot` (src/render/folk/FolkView.ts:329) → Actors `setLod` → `updateVisibility` → **`updateHiddenSkin`** (the largest self time: 19 of 135 samples, and 23 of 66), plus `compileAsync` via `stage.prepare` (31 and 11 samples), and in the first gap a texture upload (`copyExternalImageToTexture` 14 samples, preceded by a 6.9 ms `Decode Image`).
  - That is the dressing path: `FolkView.dress` → `ch.set(...)` → `prepareSlot`. prepareSlot steps through every detail level (`setLod(k)`, `stage.prepare`) and then `setLod('auto')`. Each `setLod` runs `updateHiddenSkin`, and the promise chain runs back to back without giving a frame back.
  - **GC: not the cause.** 104 MinorGCs in 10 s, 60 ms in total, the largest 1.5 ms. No MajorGC.
  - **GPU process: not the cause.** CrGpuMain is busy 14 ms over a 71 ms gap (5 ms per normal frame). Its longest tasks are ~3-4 ms WebGPU `CommandBufferStub::OnAsyncFlush`. No long SwapBuffers, Present or SyncToken waits. Viz/compositor is under 1.2 ms per gap.
  - Normal frames are bound by the main thread: renderer main is busy 9.4 ms per 10.1 ms frame, while the GPU main thread is busy 5.0 ms.
- **Pattern:** neither GC, GPU, compositor nor a timer. It is a one-off main-thread task each time a townsperson is dressed: a worker message resolves the dressing, then microtasks run updateHiddenSkin per detail level, the compile and a texture upload in one go. How often it happens depends on folk churn (the probe saw 1 dressing per 5 s while standing still).
- Possible fixes (not done): yield to the frame between detail levels in prepareSlot (one level per frame, as SHADERS.md asks of compiles), or avoid `updateHiddenSkin` for the levels being warmed. Move the skin texture decode/upload off the frame (createImageBitmap is already async; the 14 ms is the GPU copy on the main thread).
